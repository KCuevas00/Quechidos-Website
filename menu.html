The Taquiza

$18 per guest — minimum 35 people, 2 hours of service

Served with all of the following:

  • Choose 3 proteins: asada (steak)
  • Chorizo (Mexican sausage)
  • Al pastor (marinated pork & pineapple)
  • Pollo asado (chicken)
  • Carne molida (ground beef)
  • Onions, limes & cilantro
  • Green salsa
  • Red salsa

Corn quesadillas available — 4 quesadillas for an extra price.

El Pachanguero

$15 per person — minimum 40 people, 1.5 hours of service

A snack table featuring:

  • Watermelon & cantaloupe
  • An assortment of chips
  • Nuts & gummies
  • 2 aguas locas (tamarind & lime)
  • Corn in a cup with mayonnaise
  • Cheese & tajín

Toppings: chamoy, cueritos y elote.

Little Taste of Mexico

$20 per guest — minimum 35 people, 2 hours of service

Includes steak & chicken fajitas — marinated with onions, green peppers, jalapeños and tomatoes, sautéed to perfection — plus chicharrón en salsa verde y costillitas en salsa roja (pork rinds & ribs, slow-cooked in our homemade green or red salsa).

BYO Taco Bar

$16 per person — minimum 30 people, drop-off catering only

Choose 2 proteins: asada (steak), al pastor, chorizo, pollo, or carne molida. Topped with cilantro, onion & lemons.

Add-ons available: lettuce, tomato, cheese & cream, churros, corn on the cob, fruit, rice, beans.

Elote Bar
